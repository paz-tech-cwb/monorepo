Admin: how should Formulários be organized?

Eight forms with role-scoped visibility. Pick the navigation model.

A

Single "Formulários" section, hub page lists all forms

Sidebar has one entry: Formulários. Clicking it shows a hub with cards for each of the 8 forms (Cadastro do Membro, Conversão, Relatório de LG, etc.). Each card → list view of that form's submissions. Cursos lives as its own sidebar entry under Configurações.

Pros: sidebar stays clean; scales if more forms come.
Cons: one extra click to reach any form's list.

B

"Formulários" expandable group with each form as sub-item

Sidebar has Formulários as a collapsible group; expanding shows the 8 forms as sub-entries. One click to any form's list.

Pros: fastest access; clear at-a-glance inventory.
Cons: long sidebar; visually heavy when expanded.

C

Grouped by purpose: Cadastros / Relatórios / Outros

Sidebar shows three groups under Formulários: Cadastros (Membro, Conversão, Convidado), Relatórios (LG semanal, Setor, Área, Culto), Outros (Multiplicação). Each group expands to its forms.

Pros: matches mental model (capturing data vs reporting activity); easier to scan.
Cons: two-level nav; group taxonomy is opinionated.
